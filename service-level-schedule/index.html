Service Level Schedule

Effective November 2, 2026. This Service Level Schedule applies to Orders signed on or after its effective date. Download PDF

This Service Level Schedule is part of the Agreement between The Boom Company (dba Boom Logic) (“Provider”) and the Client identified in the Order, under the Master Services Agreement. It states the service levels for the Services in the Order. Each service level applies only to Services the Order includes; for example, backup service levels apply only where the Order includes backup Services. An Order may state different or additional service levels.

1. Definitions

1.1 Security Incident

“Security Incident” means unauthorized access to, or compromise of, a Client system, account, or data within the scope of the managed security Services in the Order, as confirmed by Provider’s security operations center (“SOC”) or reported by Client and confirmed by the SOC. The SOC is Provider’s internal security operations team, available twenty-four (24) hours a day, every day, working with CrowdStrike Falcon Complete managed detection and response and, for Clients on legacy monitoring, Kaseya security information and event management. Alerts, blocked attempts, and events determined to be false positives are not Security Incidents. This definition is used only for this Schedule; the Business Associate Agreement and Data Processing Agreement use their own definitions for reporting purposes.

1.2 Covered Systems

“Covered Systems” are the users, endpoints, servers, cloud tenants, and network devices identified in the Order on which Provider’s monitoring, security, and backup agents or integrations have been deployed and remain enabled. During onboarding, Provider will reconcile its remote monitoring and management inventory to the Order and report any discrepancies to Client in writing.

1.3 Tier-1 Systems

“Tier-1 Systems” are up to five (5) Covered Systems (or a higher number stated in the Order) that Client designates in writing as most critical to its operations, such as domain controllers, file servers, and the workstations that run clinical or front-desk operations. Provider will confirm the designation during onboarding and at each quarterly business review.

1.4 Response and Measurement

“Response” means a Provider technician has acknowledged the request, begun work, and communicated with Client’s contact through the ticket, telephone, or e-mail. Times in Section 2 are measured from the SOC’s confirmation of a Security Incident, including one that Client reports. All other times are measured from the earlier of Provider’s detection or Client’s report through Provider’s support channels. Business-hours targets run only during Business Hours as defined in the Master Services Agreement.

2. Security Incident Commitments

For each Security Incident, twenty-four (24) hours a day, every day, Provider will:

  • begin triage within fifteen (15) minutes;
  • notify Client’s designated contact within thirty (30) minutes; and
  • begin containment within one (1) hour, including, as the SOC determines appropriate, isolating affected endpoints, disabling or resetting compromised credentials, revoking active sessions, and blocking malicious traffic or senders.

Client authorizes Provider to take those containment actions without prior approval where Provider reasonably determines that delay would materially increase the risk of harm, and acknowledges that containment may interrupt access to affected systems. Provider will notify Client’s designated contact of each containment action as soon as practicable and in any event within eight (8) hours after each action. Provider’s incident response for each Security Incident includes investigation and containment, eradication of the threat from Covered Systems, and restoration under Section 3, and a written incident summary within five (5) business days after the incident is closed. Incident response under this Section and Section 3 is included up to twenty (20) technician hours per Security Incident and forty (40) technician hours in total per contract year (or higher numbers stated in the Order); additional hours are billed at the rates in the Order, and Provider will notify Client before exceeding the included hours. Breach-notification planning, legal advice, formal forensic investigation intended for litigation or regulatory submission, and rebuilding of systems that are not Covered Systems are Project Services, which Provider will quote promptly on request and will begin at Client’s direction without waiting for a signed Order during an active incident.

3. Ransomware and Disaster Recovery

Where the Order includes backup Services, after a ransomware event or other event that makes Covered Systems unusable, Provider will:

  • begin restoration within two (2) hours after containment;
  • restore the first Tier-1 System, in the priority order Client has designated, from the most recent verified, uncompromised backup within four (4) hours after containment (the “Recovery Time Objective”), and the remaining Tier-1 Systems in that order with continuous effort; and
  • restore all other affected Covered Systems with continuous effort, prioritized with Client.

The Recovery Time Objective assumes that restoration can be performed to existing or replacement hardware or cloud resources that are available and functioning, and it applies to a Tier-1 System only where the Order includes a local backup copy or a cloud recovery option for that system; for other Tier-1 Systems, restoration time depends on the data volume and Client’s Internet bandwidth, and Provider will restore them with continuous effort. Restoration of Microsoft 365 or other SaaS data is from the SaaS backup Service in the Order, at the restore speed the platform supports. Recovery point depends on the backup frequency and retention stated in the Order.

Outside Business Hours, work on P1 issues other than Security Incidents is included where the Order includes after-hours support; otherwise it is billable as Supplemental Services. The P1 Response target applies in either case.

6. Reporting and Reviews

Provider will deliver a monthly report covering tickets and Response performance against this Schedule, patch status, backup verification and test-restore results, and Security Incidents. Provider will hold a business review with Client each quarter, covering service performance, open recommendations under Section 5.4 of the Master Services Agreement, and the Tier-1 System designation.

7. Exclusions

A service level is not missed to the extent the failure results from: (a) systems, users, or locations that are not Covered Systems, or on which Client has disabled or removed Provider’s agents or revoked Provider’s access; (b) Client’s failure to provide access, credentials, information, or approvals reasonably requested by Provider, or Client’s instruction not to take an action; (c) the acts or omissions of Client, its users, or third parties not acting on Provider’s behalf, other than attackers in a Security Incident; (d) failures of Client’s Internet service, power, or equipment that is outside the Services or that Provider has identified in writing as unsupported; (e) outages of a Third-Party Service that Provider could not reasonably have prevented or worked around; (f) scheduled maintenance; or (g) Force Majeure.

8. Remedies

8.1 Service Credits

If Provider misses a Security Incident commitment in Section 2 or the Recovery Time Objective in Section 3, Client is entitled, on written request within thirty (30) days after the incident, to a credit of ten percent (10%) of that month’s recurring Service Fees for the affected Services. If Provider meets fewer than ninety percent (90%) of the P1 and P2 Response targets in Section 4 in a calendar month, Client is entitled to a credit of five percent (5%) of that month’s recurring Service Fees for the affected Services. Total credits in a month will not exceed twenty-five percent (25%) of that month’s recurring Service Fees. Credits are applied to the next invoice or, after termination, refunded.

8.2 Chronic Failure

Client may terminate the affected Services, or the entire Order if the affected Services are managed security or backup Services, on written notice and without an early termination fee, if within any rolling six (6) month period: (a) Provider misses a Security Incident commitment in Section 2 on two (2) or more occasions; (b) Provider fails to meet the Section 4 credit threshold in three (3) or more months; or (c) Provider misses the Recovery Time Objective and the miss was caused by Provider’s failure to perform the Services as agreed. Client must give notice within sixty (60) days after the event that gives rise to the right.

8.3 Effect

Credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are not an estimate of Client’s damages. Credits and the termination right in Section 8.2 do not limit Client’s other remedies for losses caused by Provider’s breach of the Agreement, subject to the Master Services Agreement, but any credits paid will be deducted from damages awarded for the same failure.
