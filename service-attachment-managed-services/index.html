Service Attachment for Managed Services

Effective November 2, 2026. This Service Attachment supersedes prior versions for Orders signed on or after its effective date. Download PDF

This Service Attachment is part of the Agreement between The Boom Company (dba Boom Logic) (“Provider,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) and the Client identified in the Order (“Client,” “you,” or “your”) under the Master Services Agreement. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in the Master Services Agreement.

1. Services

1.1 Scope

Provider will deliver the managed Services itemized in the Order, as described in the Schedule of Services, at the service levels in the Service Level Schedule. Additional Services may be added by a new Order or a signed change to the Order.

1.2 Help Desk

Help desk support is available during Business Hours by telephone, e-mail, and the client portal. Security Incident response is available at all times under the Service Level Schedule. Other after-hours support is included where the Order says so; otherwise it is available for critical outages as Supplemental Services at the rates in the Order.

1.3 On-site Support

Provider will provide support remotely where it can resolve the issue that way. Provider will provide on-site support at Client’s locations when an issue cannot reasonably be resolved remotely (typically hardware, cabling, or network connectivity issues) and as otherwise stated in the Order. Where the Order describes on-site support as included or unlimited, it is included for issues within the scope of the Services at the locations listed in the Order, during Business Hours, without travel charges.

1.4 Onboarding

Provider will onboard Client’s environment as described in the Order, including deploying agents, documenting the environment, and confirming Tier-1 Systems. Any onboarding fee is stated in the Order; an onboarding fee waived in the Order is not charged later, including if the Order ends early.

2. Credentials and Licenses

2.1 Client Credentials

Administrative credentials for Client’s systems, tenants, domains, and devices are Client Configuration Records and belong to Client. Client will remain the owner of record of its Microsoft 365 (or other productivity) tenant, its domain registrations and DNS, and the administrator accounts for its firewalls and clinical or line-of-business applications, and may maintain its own emergency (“break-glass”) administrator accounts. Client will tell Provider when it uses an emergency account, so that Provider can distinguish authorized use from a Security Incident.

2.2 Provider Tool Credentials

Credentials that Provider issues for access to Provider’s own tools, consoles, and portals are Provider’s Confidential Information, may not be shared with third parties without Provider’s consent, and expire at the end of the Agreement.

2.3 Licenses

Provider grants Client the right to use the software and services specified in the Order during its term. Licenses Provider procures through its distribution or partner programs may be held in Provider’s partner tenant where the vendor requires it; Provider will transfer or help Client re-procure them on termination.

3. Client Environment

3.1 Minimum Standards

Client’s covered environment must meet the following minimum standards, or Client will authorize upgrades to meet them (“Minimum Standards”):

  • servers and workstations run operating system versions supported by their publishers;
  • software is genuine, licensed, and supported by its publisher;
  • a currently licensed, supported firewall sits between the internal network and the Internet;
  • Provider has reliable remote access to covered systems; and
  • for health care Clients, an identity-management system (such as Microsoft Entra ID or Active Directory) is in place, and for Clients that process payment cards, payment systems are segregated from other networks.

Costs of meeting the Minimum Standards are not included unless the Order says so. Provider will identify gaps in writing during onboarding and propose a remediation plan.

3.2 Failure to Meet Minimum Standards

If Client’s environment stops meeting the Minimum Standards, Provider will notify Client in writing, describing the gap and the recommended remedy. Service levels do not apply to the affected systems until the gap is remedied. If the gap is not remedied within sixty (60) days and it materially prevents Provider from delivering the Services securely, Provider may suspend the Services for the affected systems on fifteen (15) days’ further written notice; Provider will not suspend Security Incident response for an active Security Incident.

3.3 Hardware Equipment; Legacy Equipment

Client equipment should be under a manufacturer warranty or support contract. Provider, in its reasonable opinion supported by manufacturer information, may designate equipment as obsolete, defective, or past its useful service life and recommend its replacement in writing, including by e-mail or in a support ticket (a “Replacement Recommendation”). Equipment becomes “Legacy Equipment” if (a) Provider has issued a Replacement Recommendation for it and it has been the subject of three (3) or more support incidents requiring repair or remediation after that date, or (b) it is no longer covered by manufacturer warranty or support and Provider has designated it in writing as Legacy Equipment. Support, repair, or remediation of Legacy Equipment is a Supplemental Service billable at the hourly rate in the Order (or, if none, Provider’s then-current rate), with a one-hour minimum, plus parts. Provider will identify the billable status of the work in the ticket before performing it, and Client may decline the work. Legacy Equipment returns to covered scope when it is replaced with equipment meeting the Minimum Standards. Provider is not responsible for downtime, data loss, or security exposure to the extent caused by Client’s continued use of Legacy Equipment or equipment subject to a Replacement Recommendation, except to the extent caused by Provider’s failure to perform the Services as agreed. Security monitoring and backup Services continue to apply to Legacy Equipment for as long as the equipment can support Provider’s agents.

3.4 Network Changes

Client will notify Provider by e-mail or ticket of significant proposed network changes and give Provider a reasonable opportunity to comment. Provider is not responsible for failures to the extent caused by changes Client made without notice. Evaluation of change requests that require significant research, design, or testing is billable as Project Services.

3.5 Before Major Changes

Before installing or making a major change to a server, application, or network that could put data at risk, Provider will confirm that a current, restorable backup of the affected systems exists (where backup Services are in the Order) or will tell Client that one does not, so that Client can decide whether to proceed.

4. Provider-Supplied Equipment

Where the Order includes Equipment on a rental basis, the recurring Service Fee for that Equipment includes the use of the Equipment and the labor needed to install and maintain it. Client will use the Equipment only for its intended purpose, will not allow anyone other than Provider to service or move it, and will not connect third-party accessories to it without Provider’s consent (which will not be unreasonably withheld). Provider will provide initial training on newly installed Equipment. Provider-owned software installed on Equipment remains Provider’s property.

5. Exclusions

The following are not included in the Services unless the Order says so, and are available as Supplemental Services or Project Services:

  • development or modification of software or web sites;
  • implementation of new or replacement line-of-business software, and maintenance of line-of-business application content (Provider will coordinate with the application vendor);
  • office relocations and new site build-outs;
  • equipment refreshes other than workstation provisioning included in the Order;
  • printer hardware repair;
  • devices, home computers, and locations that are not identified in the Order;
  • formal training programs beyond the security awareness training in the Order;
  • the cost of hardware, parts, software, licensing, and third-party vendor support fees; and
  • handling of billing disputes with third-party vendors (Provider will continue to handle technical issues with those vendors).

Provider is not responsible for a failure to provide the Services to the extent caused by: the lack of manufacturer warranty or support for Client’s equipment; modifications to systems by anyone other than Provider; loss of Internet connectivity, power, or UPS at Client’s sites; Client’s reprioritization of tasks; Client’s failure to meet its obligations under the Agreement; or Force Majeure. These exclusions do not apply to the extent the condition results from Provider’s failure to perform the Services as agreed.

6. Service Fees

6.1 Monthly Inventory

Provider will conduct a monthly inventory of the users, devices, locations, and other units covered by the Services (“Service Units”). Additions are invoiced from the month in which they are added; inactive users and devices are removed as provided in Section 3.2 of the Master Services Agreement. Billed Service Units will not fall below the Minimum Commitment in Section 3.2 of the Master Services Agreement.

6.2 Backup Storage

Backup Services include the storage allowance stated in the Order. Provider will give Client at least thirty (30) days’ written notice before any storage overage charge first applies, together with options to reduce usage (for example, by adjusting retention or excluding non-business data). Overage is billed at the rate in the Order.

6.3 Setup Fees

Any setup or onboarding fee is stated in the Order and invoiced as stated there.

7. Term and Termination

7.1 Term and Renewal

This Service Attachment applies from the service start date in the Order for the Initial Term and any Renewal Term, as provided in Section 4.2 of the Master Services Agreement.

7.2 Early Termination by Client for Convenience

No sooner than ninety (90) days after the service start date, Client may elect to end the Services under this Service Attachment for convenience during the Initial Term on sixty (60) days’ written notice, as an alternative to completing the Initial Term, by paying an early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the recurring Service Fees that would have been payable from the effective date of termination through the end of the Initial Term, calculated using the Minimum Commitment quantities and the rates in effect on the date of notice. The Parties agree that this fee reflects Provider’s unrecovered onboarding costs, waived fees, committed third-party licenses, and staffing for the Initial Term, and is a fair price for the option to end early. No early termination fee applies in the circumstances listed in Section 4.4 of the Master Services Agreement or during a Renewal Term.

7.3 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate for cause as provided in Section 4.3 of the Master Services Agreement.

7.4 Effect of Termination

On termination, Provider will provide transition assistance and deliver Client Data and Client Configuration Records as provided in Sections 4.6 and 13.1 of the Master Services Agreement and, for PHI, Section 5.3 of the Business Associate Agreement. Provider will remove its agents and software from Client’s devices on the cut-over date agreed with Client, or by the Exit Date (as defined in Section 13.1 of the Master Services Agreement) if none is agreed, and Client will reasonably cooperate. Client will pay Service Fees through the effective date of termination and any third-party termination charges that Client approved when it ordered the affected third-party services or that are stated in the Order.
